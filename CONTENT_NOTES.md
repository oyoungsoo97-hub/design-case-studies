# Content and asset scope

This is a public collection of design explanations, not a source release of an employer’s product. The SVG diagrams were authored for these case studies and contain no product logos, customer images, private UI screenshots or private source files. They are conceptual diagrams, not before/after screenshots.

Names and links identify work already presented in the linked portfolio. They do not imply company endorsement. No blanket open-source license is granted for third-party brands or materials on linked sites. Existing rights remain with their respective owners.

Public summaries were prepared from retained project records on November 11, 2026. The source archive and detailed evidence remain private. Findings are scoped to the documented prototypes; planned user research is not presented as completed research.
